The name pretty much says it all. It took Lo Esencial a while to launch their first 
Tote because despite being a basic, it is an object that was exhaustively designed from 
a perspective of utility and daily practice. They wanted to create the perfect Tote: slim, 
spacious, comfortable, without excess straps, with practical details, different modes 
of use, iconic in its form and extremely attractive. The result is the Minimal Tote.
Flat style. Slim design, allowing better management and organization of the objects 
that you carry daily in it, keeping its shape.

Room for everything . The size is perfect to carry a laptop, some books and everything
you need in your day to day. In addition, we efficiently distribute every centimeter, placing 
2 internal pockets of 20x15cm, perfect for keeping small objects at hand.

Made to last. Solid construction, made entirely by hand in genuine leather with vegetable 
tanning and details in natural wool and copper.

Wear it however you prefer Whether with its unique adjustable shoulder strap, or with its
short handles for briefcase carry, our Minimal Tote is perfect for efficient and comfortable 
use in multiple ways.

Packed with details Top magnetic closure, internal key hook, adjustable strap, solid copper
rivets… Every detail will make it your ultimate bag.

Materials: Genuine Leather with vegetable tanning, natural wool, copper

Size: 42cm x 42cm
